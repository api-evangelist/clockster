For "Aiplus," discipline is not just a word but one of the most crucial aspects of the educational environment. Given that the center focuses on educating children, every employee must set an example of discipline. Violetta Kim explains this by saying, "We are an educational center, which means that children come for lessons, and, accordingly, all employees should be here by that time, especially teachers, mentors, and administrators."
