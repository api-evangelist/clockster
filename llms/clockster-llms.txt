# Clockster

> Clockster is a frontline staff-management super-app for food & beverage and retail businesses, combining attendance and time tracking, shift scheduling, automated payroll, earned wage access, business communication, tasks and checklists in one mobile-first platform. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Singapore with offices in Kazakhstan and Indonesia, Clockster serves 1,300+ SMEs across Central and Southeast Asia and is backed by 500 Global, I2BF Global Ventures and Quest Ventures. Clockster does not currently publish a public developer API; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public catalog.
